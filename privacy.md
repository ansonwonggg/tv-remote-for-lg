# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 25, 2026*

TV Remote for LG ("the app") does not collect, share or sell any personal data. The developer receives no information about you or how you use the app.

## What the app stores, and where

All of the following stays on your iPhone:

- **The TVs you add**: their name, IP address and network (MAC) address, saved in the app's local storage so you can reconnect and turn the TV on.
- **The pairing key** your TV issues when you accept the connection, saved in the iPhone Keychain.
- **Your app shortcuts**: the apps you pin to the remote screen.

Deleting a TV in the app removes its saved details and pairing key. Deleting the app removes its local storage.

## Network use

The app talks only to TVs on your local network, to find them, send remote-control commands and wake them. It does not contact any server run by the developer or by third parties, and it contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

iOS asks for **Local Network** permission so the app can find your TV. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
