# TV Remote for LG

An iPhone remote control for LG webOS smart TVs on your home Wi-Fi.

*TV Remote for LG is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by LG Electronics. LG and webOS are trademarks of LG Electronics.*

## Getting started

1. Connect your iPhone to the same Wi-Fi network as the TV, and turn the TV on.
2. Open the app and tap **Allow** when iOS asks to find devices on your local network.
3. Tap your TV under **On this network** (or add it by IP address with **+**).
4. Accept the connection prompt that appears on the TV.

## Power On doesn't work

On the TV, turn on:

- **Settings → All Settings → General → Quick Start+**
- **Settings → All Settings → Connection → Mobile Connection Management → TV On With Mobile → Turn on via Wi-Fi**

The exact menu names vary by TV model and firmware. Connect to the TV once while it is on, so the app can learn the details it needs to wake it.

## The app can't find my TV

- Make sure the TV is fully on, not in standby, and on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone.
- Check **iPhone Settings → TV Remote → Local Network** is on.
- Pull down on the TV list to scan again, or add the TV by its IP address.
